Straight Answers

The fair questions.

"Every lead company says exclusive."

They do. The difference is whether they can prove it or only promise it. When the homeowner comes out of your own ad account, there's no shared pool for them to come from.

"What counts as a appointment set?"

You and a phone-verified homeowner, on the line together, for at least two minutes. It's logged with a timestamp and a recording you can pull up any time — so neither of us is taking the other's word for it. Voicemails, wrong numbers and no-answers are free.

"How do I know I won't get buried in calls?"

You can pause us any day, for any reason, with no contract to get out of. And you'll see every appointment set as it happens rather than at the end of the month, so nothing on the invoice is a surprise.

"I don't have $2–3k a month for ads."

Then this isn't the right fit yet, and we'll tell you that instead of taking your time. This works for shops already doing enough volume to fund the account.
