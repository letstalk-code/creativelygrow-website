Privacy Policy

Plain English: what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Creatively Grow ("we", "us") is based in St. Petersburg, Florida. This policy covers information you give us through creativelygrow.com, our Facebook and Instagram forms, phone calls, and text messages.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, phone number, email address and business name.
  • Answers you give on our forms, such as the type of business you run and roughly how many customers you have.
  • Messages and call notes from our conversations with you.
  • Basic website analytics, such as pages visited. These don't identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and put together anything you asked for, like a Client Comeback Check.
  • To schedule calls and send reminders.
  • To provide our services if you become a client.

We don't sell your information.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number and agree to be contacted, we may send text messages about your request, your appointments, and our services. Some of these messages may be automated. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of purchase.

We do not share or sell your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Who else handles it

We use trusted service providers to run our business. They include our CRM and messaging platform (LeadConnector / HighLevel), Meta (when you use a Facebook or Instagram form), and our website host. They only process your information to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we're in contact or working together, and as needed for our records. Ask us and we'll delete it, unless we have to keep it by law.

Your choices

  • Opt out of texts anytime by replying STOP.
  • Ask us what we have on file, or ask us to correct or delete it.
