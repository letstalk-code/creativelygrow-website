Questions we get

Do we need a script first?

No. Most of these work better unscripted. We build a question list from your brief and interview your people. If you already have written SOPs, send them and we will pull the structure from those.

Can our own people be on camera?

They should be. Your team believes a supervisor they know far more than a hired actor. Nobody needs to be polished, that is our job in the edit.

How long does it take?

A single video is usually a week from shoot to delivery. A full series is two to four weeks, and the longest part is normally your internal approvals, not the edit.

What if the process changes later?

If it is a graphic or a line of narration, that is a small update. If the process itself changed on the floor, that section gets reshot, and we bill the day and the video like any other.

Who owns the footage?

You do. It is your company, your people and your process. You get the finished videos, and the raw footage on request.
