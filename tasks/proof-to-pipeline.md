## The problem

**H2:** Great work doesn't sell itself. Proof does.

Your best jobs happen where no one sees them: in a customer's attic, on a roof, in a
treatment room. Then a stranger compares you to three others on Google, sees the same
stock photos everyone uses, and picks the cheapest.

And when someone *does* choose you, the call goes to voicemail, nobody texts back, and
they book the next name on the list.

You don't have a marketing problem. You have a **proof** problem and a **leak** problem.

---

## The method: three steps

### 01 · Prove
**We film the real thing.**
A brand story film shot on location: your space, your people, your customers. The same
crew that shot Coca-Cola, Publix, and Cornerstone Air. Then monthly content, shot by us
or made with AI, so the proof keeps coming.

### 02 · Capture
**We put the proof where buyers decide.**
Your Google Business Profile, a landing page built around the film, video and static
ads, Google Ads. Wherever someone is comparing you, your proof shows up first.

### 03 · Follow up
**We make sure no one slips through.**
Missed call? Texted back in seconds, from your number. Booked? A review request goes out
every time, so the next stranger sees even more proof. The loop feeds itself.

**Pull line:** Prove → Capture → Follow up. Each step makes the next one work better.
