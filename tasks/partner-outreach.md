# Agency Partner Outreach — messages (2026-08-07)

Channel: **email first, text second, call last.** These are partner-development conversations, not
cold sales calls — a written note lets them forward it to whoever owns fulfillment, which is what you
actually want.

**The frame, in one line:** *I'm your camera department.* You are not selling them marketing. You are
removing a fulfillment problem they already have. Never pitch strategy, SEO, or systems to an agency —
that's their business and it reads as competition.

**The offer:** $695 half-day shoot (up to 4 hrs), raw files delivered, they keep the client
relationship entirely. Editing quoted separately if they want it. Trade rate, not retail.

**Three rules:**
1. Never contact their client. Say so explicitly — it's the #1 thing a partner fears.
2. Don't name-drop brands as a flex; name them as proof you won't embarrass them on set.
3. No urgency, no scarcity, no "circling back." These are peers.
