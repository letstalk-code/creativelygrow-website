## The position, in one line
**Hire a creative director. Get the whole team.**

**Two rules from Devon (2026-11-06):**
1. **A business creative director, not a film one.** We direct how the whole business looks,
   sounds and shows up: brand, website, content, Google, ads, customer follow-up. Video is one
   tool, not the headline.
2. **The company, not a person.** Creatively Grow *is* the creative director. Copy says "we" and
   "your creative director", never "I'm Devon." If the brand is one person, everyone wants that
   person and the company can't grow past him.

Why it's a different position, not a niche (the Roel Hannen video): everyone else stands on the
same spot ("marketing agency, we get you results, here are our numbers"). We sell a *role* a
business already understands, a creative director for the whole business, with a team behind it.

**Words we use:** creative director, team, creative team, direction, the work, sit-down, customers.
**Words we never use in ads/pages:** marketing, agency, campaign, leads, funnel, growth system,
ROI, "10x", any dollar promise.

## 1. Landing page (`/creative-director`, preview only)

**Hero**
- Eyebrow: Creatively Grow · Tampa Bay
- H1: **Hire a creative director. Get the whole team.**
- Sub: Most businesses don't need another vendor. They need someone who owns how the whole
  business looks, sounds and shows up, and a team that makes it happen. That's what Creatively
  Grow is: your creative director.
- Side panel: "Your creative director for: your brand, your website, your content, your ads,
  every customer call."
- Button: **Book a Sit-Down** · secondary: See the Work

**What a creative director actually does**
- *Sets the direction.* You stop guessing what to say, build, post or fix. Your creative director makes the call and tells you why.
- *Keeps it one look.* Your website, content, ads and Google profile finally look like the same business.
- *Runs the team.* You don't hire, brief or chase vendors. We do.
- *Shows up.* Two or three sit-downs a month with your creative director. A person, not a ticket system.

**The team** (heading: "One creative director. The whole team behind it.")
- Brand: what you say, how you look, why customers should pick you
- Website: your site and the pages your ads point to
- Content: photo, video and posts, real or AI-made, every month
- Google & search: so people find you
- Ads: the right work in front of the right customers
- Customer follow-up: AI receptionist, missed-call text-back, review requests

**How a month works:** Sit-down → Direction (your creative director sends the month's plan in
plain English) → The team makes it → You approve → It goes live, and we watch the phone.

**What we won't say** (the differentiator)
- H2: **No promises you've heard before.** ("We'll add $5,000 a month", "a million dollars in ad
  spend", "guaranteed results", crossed out)
- Nobody can honestly promise you numbers before they've seen your business. What we can promise
  is the job: a creative director who shows up, a team that delivers, and a business you're proud
  to put your name on.

**The work comes first:** websites, content and customer follow-up for Tampa Bay businesses like
Cornerstone Air and Steadfast Plumbing, and brand work for names like Coca-Cola and Publix.
[Website showcase video] [Cornerstone Air spotlight]

**How we're built:** Creatively Grow is built around one role: the creative director. Every client
gets a dedicated one, who learns your business, sets the direction and runs a team of specialists
behind it. You never manage vendors, and you never explain your business twice.

**Who it's for:** businesses where a customer starts with a call or a booking: dental offices,
clinics and chiropractors, law firms, contractors and home services, auto shops, restaurants.

**The honest terms:** you keep everything (website, accounts, phone number, every customer in
it). Your ad money stays on your card. [Price: Devon's call.]

**Closing:** **Let's sit down.** Twenty minutes. Tell us what you're working on and we'll tell
you what we'd do first. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.

## 2. Three ads (Meta: Facebook + Instagram)

Same audience (Tampa Bay business owners, 30–65), same budget split, so only the angle differs.
Each has a still image and a short video. **No one person is the face of the ad.** Videos use
voice over, on-screen text, or a team member, never "I'm Devon."

### Ad A: "The Role"
- **Primary text:** Big brands have a creative director deciding how the whole business looks,
  sounds and shows up. Your business deserves the same. Creatively Grow is your creative
  director, team included.
- **Headline:** Hire a creative director. Get the whole team.
- **Image:** a creative director's brand board: brand, website, Google profile, content, ads and
  follow-up laid out as one system. Text: "YOUR CREATIVE DIRECTOR".
- **Video (20s):** *"Coca-Cola has a creative director. So does Publix. Your business probably
  doesn't, so you're the one deciding what to say, what to post, what to fix on the website.
  That's our job. Creatively Grow is your creative director: we set the direction, our team makes
  it, and you get your time back. Let's sit down."*

### Ad B: "The Team"
- **Primary text:** Brand. Website. Content. Ads. Customer follow-up. You could hire and manage
  five vendors, or you could have one creative director who runs them all. You talk to one
  person. We handle the rest.
- **Headline:** One director. The whole team.
- **Image:** five team cards (brand, website, content, ads, follow-up) plus a sixth, highlighted:
  "Your creative director".
- **Video (25s):** quick cuts (brand board, website build, content shoot, ad going live, a
  text-back landing). VO: *"You don't need five vendors who've never met. You need one creative
  director. Ours runs the whole team: your brand, your website, your content, your ads, your
  follow-up. One direction. One team."*

### Ad C: "No Promises You've Heard Before"
- **Primary text:** You've heard it: "We'll add $5,000 a month." "We've spent a million on ads."
  We're not going to say any of that. Nobody can promise you numbers before they've seen your
  business. What we'll promise is the job: a creative director who shows up and a team that
  delivers.
- **Headline:** No pitch. Just a creative director.
- **Image:** the familiar lines crossed out in red ("10x your revenue", "$1M in ad spend",
  "guaranteed results"), then "A creative director who shows up."
- **Video (20s):** plain set, VO or on-screen text: *"Every ad you see promises you more money.
  We're not going to do that. We don't know your business yet. Here's what we'll do: sit down
  with you, set the direction, and have our team make it happen. That's the job. Let's talk."*

## 4. How we'd test it (once Devon says go)
1. Turn on Vercel Analytics first (one click), so we can see visits.
2. Put the landing page live at `/creative-director` (not in the menu at first).
3. Run A, B, C side by side for 2–3 weeks at a budget Devon picks, with the money split evenly.
4. The scoreboard is **booked sit-downs**, not clicks. Kill the loser at the end of week 2 and put its money into the winner.
5. Then update the site wording (section 3) to match whichever angle won.
